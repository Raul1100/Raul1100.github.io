RFM segments actual 90-day churn

RFM scores recency, frequency and spend against everyone else. The model also compares each customer with their own rhythm and order size, which is where most of its extra lift comes from.

How it works

520 simulated B2B customers with their own ordering rhythms; about 45% eventually leave, slowing down and placing smaller orders first, and some order lumpily without leaving. A snapshot is taken every 30 days: seven features from history up to that day, label = "no order in the next 90 days". Training uses only snapshots whose label window closes before the test snapshot (time-based split, no leakage). Logistic regression with L2 is trained by gradient descent in your browser.
