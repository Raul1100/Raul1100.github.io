How it works

data is generated in the browser (trend, yearly seasonality, a festive-season bump for cutlery, random promotions and noise). The models are hand-written in JavaScript: naive, seasonal naive, an 8-week moving average, additive Holt-Winters (grid-searched α/β/γ on one-step error), ridge regression on lag and calendar features with recursive multi-step forecasting, and an HW + ridge ensemble. Source: demos/demand-forecasting/core.js.
