How it works

24 months of simulated B2B orders (60 customers, 10 products, 8 states) with seasonality, growth and churn. Revenue is aggregated monthly; the forecast is additive Holt-Winters (12-month season, α/β/γ grid-searched on one-step error) with a ±1.28σ band from in-sample residuals. RFM uses quintile scores. The question box is a small keyword parser that maps a question to a query over the same data. It is deliberately not an LLM, so every answer is exact.
