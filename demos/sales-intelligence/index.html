How it works

24 months of simulated B2B orders from 53 food-service accounts (café and QSR outlets, cloud kitchens, ice-cream and bubble-tea chains, caterers, canteens, hotels, an inflight kitchen and three distributors) across 9 states and 11 SKUs. Each kind of buyer orders the products it really uses at list price less a small trade discount, with summer and festive seasonality, segment growth and a few lost accounts. Revenue is aggregated monthly; the forecast is additive Holt-Winters (12-month season, α/β/γ grid-searched on one-step error) with a ±1.28σ band from in-sample residuals. RFM uses quintile scores. The question box is a small keyword parser that maps a question to a query over the same data. It is deliberately not an LLM, so every answer is exact.
