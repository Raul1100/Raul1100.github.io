How it works

a single-page app. Plans you type are saved per date and department in localStorage. "Actual" comes from simulated shift entries (seeded, so the history is stable). For today it only counts the part of each shift that has already run, so the numbers move during the day. Status: on track ≥ 95% of the pro-rated plan, behind 80–95%, critical below 80%.
