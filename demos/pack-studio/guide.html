BioPapro Design Hub – user guide

Design Hub makes our packaging and product documents in the browser: boxes with a 3D preview, printed wrapping paper, and TDS sheets in the same format as the TDS folder. Nothing to install; open the link and start.

1Pick the product

Every box starts from one of our products. The left panel walks you through 4 steps: Product → Box size → Design → Download. Use Next → and ← Back at the bottom, or click a step number at the top.

  1. Open the link. The first time, a short welcome card appears; click Let's start.
  2. Use the filter buttons (Paper straws, Wooden cutlery, Displays, Cartons) to narrow the list.
  3. Click a product card. The box size, artwork and colours are filled in for you. A green tick shows which one is selected.
  4. Click Next: Box size →.
TipPicking a product is only a starting point. Everything can be changed in the next steps.

2Box type & size

  1. Choose the box type: Shelf box (tuck-in lid, retail), Shipping carton (corrugated, export), Counter display (open tray with a back card), or Wrapping paper (see the Wrapping paper section).
  2. Check the inside size in millimetres: Length, Width, Height. Enter the space the product needs inside the box; the board thickness is added automatically.
  3. For straws, let it work out the size: under Auto-size for straws, enter how many straws, their thickness and length, tick paper-wrapped if they are, and click Work out the box size for me.
  4. Choose the board (white board, kraft, 3-ply or 5-ply corrugated). This changes the weight and cost figures, and the inside colour in 3D.
ImportantThe sizes in the product list are a starting point. Before sending anything to the die-maker, check the inside size against a real sample of the product.

3Design the box

  • Words on the box: brand name, product name, size line, pieces in the box (shown in the round badge) and the slogan in the bottom band.
  • Colour theme: one click changes all colours together (Eco green, Natural kraft, Café brown, and more).
  • Board colour: White, Brown kraft or Coloured. Background pattern: Plain, Leaves, Stripes or Dots.
  • Logo: click the logo box or drag a file onto it. A PNG with no background looks best.
  • Back & side panel text (click to open): features, specifications, product description, manufacturer details and the barcode number.
  • Custom colours: pick exact colours instead of a theme.
  • Use your own artwork on one side: choose a side (for example the Front) and upload a finished design made in CorelDRAW or Photoshop. Leave 3 mm extra on every edge.
Before printingThe address, FSSAI number and barcode on the back panel are examples. Replace them with the real details for the product.

Flat print layout

Click Flat print layout in the top bar (or Both to see it next to the 3D view). This is the box opened out flat, exactly as the printer and die-maker see it.

  • Panel names shows or hides the labels (FRONT, SIDE, TOP…). Fit to screen resets the view. Scroll to zoom, drag to move.
  • Flat sheet size – size of the board before folding.
  • Board per box – grams of board for one box (and boxes per kg).
  • Best printing sheet – how many boxes fit on standard printing sheets (20×30", 23×36", 25×36", 28×40").
  • Cutting / folding rule – total length of cut and fold lines; the die-maker prices the die on this.

Saving your work

  • Your latest design is remembered automatically in this browser on this computer.
  • To keep a design safely, or to send it to a colleague, use Save project (step 4). It downloads a small .json file.
  • To continue later, click Open project and choose that file.
ImportantClearing the browser history or using another computer starts fresh. Use Save project for anything you want to keep.

WWrapping paper

For individually wrapped cutlery (a printed sheet that folds around the piece) and straw wrappers (a narrow printed strip).

  1. Go to Packaging → step 2 and click Wrapping paper. Use ← Back to boxes to return.
  2. Choose Cutlery wrapper or Straw wrapper at the top.
  3. Product: pick from From your products. The sheet size is worked out for you (for a 160 mm fork: 100 × 210 mm, folds 10 | 20 | 40 | 20 | 10 mm, 10 mm seals).
  4. Sheet size: to set your own size, turn off Size automatically from product and type the width, height, seal size and fold panels (left to right, separated by commas).
  5. Paper: choose the paper (Kraft, KLD = kraft with LD/PE coating, White) and the GSM. The weight per piece updates.
  6. Print: main text and second line, ink colour, surface or reverse print, number of colours, drawing size, eye marks, and whether to show the BioPapro logo.
  7. Proof details: job ID, customer name and design name. These appear in the proof's title box.
  8. Roll & quantity: roll width and number of pieces, to see how many wrappers fit across the roll and the roll length needed.
  9. Download: Proof PDF for customer approval (it has a sign / date box), Artwork (SVG) at actual size for the printer.
TipIn the artwork file, fold and seal lines are on a separate layer called "Guides". Ask the printer to switch that layer off when printing.

The same 4 steps for every document

  1. Click the document type (four cards at the top left).
  2. Under Start from, choose an existing product. All products from the TDS folder are there (14 straws, 8 raw materials, 53 cutlery items, 6 customer specs). Click Blank for a new one.
  3. Change what you need and upload the photo(s).
  4. Click Download PDF (and Word for customer specs). The file name follows the Drive folder style, for example 8X197MM PLAIN WHITE PAPER STRAW.pdf.

Paper straw TDS

  • Fill in product name, diameter, length, ply, GSM and the weight per straw. The weight for 100 pcs is calculated.
  • The green box shows a calculated weight as a check. The sheet prints the weight you type, so enter the measured value.
  • Enter the measured inner and outer diameter. They appear on the drawing.
  • Photo: click the dashed box on the page (or drag a photo file onto it), or use Upload on the left. One photo of the straw lying flat is used at the top right and, turned upright, for the drawing.
  • Arrows: drag the red dots on the page so the arrows touch the edges of the straw. The blue dots move the line and label. The dots never print.

Raw material TDS

  • Fill in weight, pcs per kg, wastage and thickness.
  • Shape (spoon, fork, knife, scoop) decides which measurements are drawn.
  • Type each measurement in the list (head length, head width, neck width, total length, handle end width…).
  • Upload a photo of the piece lying flat with the head to the left. Use Zoom, Left/right, Up/down so the piece fills the strip; Photo strip width/height resizes the strip.
  • Drag the dots so each arrow touches the right edges. Reset arrow positions puts them back.

Finished cutlery TDS

  • Company name is pre-filled as BIOPAPRO CUTLERIES PVT. LTD.
  • Fill in thickness, pieces per packet, box weight, pieces per box and box size (L × W × H).
  • The green box checks your numbers: grams per piece, pieces per kg and packets per box.
  • Extra line is for special cases such as round sticks (1 KG = 371 PCS).
  • Upload two photos: loose pieces (top) and the packed packet (bottom).

Customer product spec

  • Header: date, customer name and product name.
  • Photos: + Add photos. The first photo sits on its own row; up to 3 more go side by side below. A red border is added automatically.
  • Details: each section (Product Details, Packaging Material, Packaging Details, Documents…) has label / value lines. Use + Add line, ✕ to remove, ↑ to move a section up, and + Add a section… for Wrapping Paper, Tissue Paper or Inner Carton details.
  • Checks: a yellow message appears if the net weight is not gross − tare, or pieces per carton is not pieces × packets. Fix the numbers before sending.
  • Download as Download PDF or Word (Word can be edited further).
Check before sendingThe product list was copied from the existing Drive files, including any mistakes in them. Two existing specs have net weights that don't add up (Rebel Foods, Bistro); the yellow check will point these out.

Moving and resizing photos (like Word)

You usually don't need to adjust anything. When you add a photo, the tool finds the product in it, trims the empty background, fits it neatly in its spot, and keeps it inside the page. On the drawing, the length lines move to the ends of the product and the width arrows move to its edges by themselves. Use the steps below only if you want to change something.

Photo won't upload?Use JPG or PNG. iPhone photos in HEIC format can't be used: on the iPhone set Camera → Formats → Most Compatible, or send the photo through WhatsApp first (that turns it into a JPG). The tool shows a message if a photo can't be read.

Taking good product photos

  • Lay the product flat on a plain grey or white sheet, horizontal, head to the left.
  • Use daylight or a bright white light; avoid strong shadows.
  • Hold the phone straight above the product, not at an angle, and fill most of the photo with the product.
  • Phone photos are fine; large photos are reduced automatically.

?Common problems

The 3D box is not showing
Reload the page. If it still doesn't show, the computer's graphics may be switched off in the browser: in Chrome go to Settings → System and turn on Use graphics acceleration when available, then restart Chrome. The flat layout and all downloads work even without 3D.
Product documents say "Loading document fonts…" for a while
The first time, the page downloads the document fonts (about 10 seconds on a slow connection). After that it opens quickly.
The printed box or proof is the wrong size
In the print window choose Actual size / 100%, not "Fit to page". The wrapping-paper proof is printed slightly smaller to fit A4; the sizes written on it and the artwork SVG are the real sizes.
Where did my download go?
Downloads go to your normal Downloads folder. In Chrome press Ctrl + J to see the list.
My design disappeared
Designs are remembered only in the same browser on the same computer, and are lost if the browser history is cleared. Always use Save project for work you want to keep.
The Word file looks a little different from the PDF
Word draws borders, bullets and spacing in its own way. Use the PDF for sending; use Word only when the customer needs to edit the spec.
A product or size is missing from the list
Start from the closest product and change the values, or use Blank. Ask the admin to add it to the product library so it's there for everyone.
